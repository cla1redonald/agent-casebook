# Statement: Fiona Rennie, planning manager

Taken 21 September 2026.

The assistant was meant to take the simple questions off the phones. For a few weeks it did. Then the calls came back, and they were nearly all the same: a resident had a garden room or an extension in mind, had a drawing or a site plan, attached it as the assistant suggested, and got "Something went wrong. Please try again later."

Residents who typed a question without a drawing tell us it works. Some tried again two or three times with the drawing and gave up.

I was told the evaluations were passing every night, so I assumed the residents were doing something wrong. I no longer think that.
