# Statement: Morag Lindsay, operations manager

Taken 29 September 2026.

The first complaint came in on 16 September. A man had driven to Ardsallow for the 07:10 and found the slipway closed. The refit notice had been on the website since 3 September. He showed the ticket office his phone: the assistant had told him the sailing was running.

We had four more like it that week, including a family with a campervan who had asked about the 07:10 on 17 September and been told it was "scheduled to run". It said it could not see the spaces and suggested they check, so they assumed space was the only question. I read some of the chats. The assistant was polite and definite about the sailings. The only thing it was ever unsure about was the spaces.

Before that it had been good. In August it told people about cancellations before we had time to put up a notice.

I asked our contractor whether anything had changed. He said the keys had been rotated on the 8th as normal and the booking site was fine. Iona had also changed the sign-off that week. The dashboard was green the whole time, which is why nobody looked.
